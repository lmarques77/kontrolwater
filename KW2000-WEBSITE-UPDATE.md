# KW2000 website update and evidence gaps

This note separates brochure-supported product facts from information still needed before stronger performance or commercial claims can be published. The source reviewed was `KW2000 Brochure (1).pdf`, supplied on 30 September 2026.

## Recommended product naming

Use this brand architecture until the commercial name is final:

- **Brand:** KONTROLWATER
- **Public product name:** **Rio**
- **Model designation:** **KW2000**
- **Descriptor:** Smart pool & garden controller

Recommended presentation: **KONTROLWATER Rio** with **Model KW2000** in technical material.

“Rio” is the strongest option: short, premium, international, easy to say, and meaning “river” gives it an authentic connection to Portugal and water flow. “Rio 1” is useful only if a numbered product family is already planned. “Rio System” is descriptive but less distinctive. Before adopting Rio publicly, run trademark, domain and competitor checks in the intended markets.

## Brochure-supported facts now used on the website

- One wall-mounted cabinet and one controller board.
- Pool, spa, water-feature and garden control in one system.
- Touchscreen control at the cabinet and remote control through a web app.
- Integrated UV disinfection.
- Integrated salt chlorination with chlorine generated on demand.
- Inline sensor manifold for live water-quality readings.
- Automatic pH correction and HCl dosing.
- Motorised valve control.
- Independent control of two pump motors.
- Pressure-sensing automatic sand-filter backwash.
- Chemical-supply level warnings.
- Pre-plumbed and pre-wired installation.
- Published envelope: 160 × 140 × 25 cm.
- Designed and manufactured in Portugal.

## Existing claims removed or replaced

The brochure does not substantiate the following previous-product claims, so they have been removed from customer-facing copy:

- 10-second measurement interval.
- ±0.01 pH dosing accuracy.
- Installation in under four hours.
- 12+ monitored parameters.
- 70% reduction in chemical spend.
- 40% energy savings.
- 99% / 99.8% system uptime.
- Predictive equipment-failure detection.
- Audit-ready compliance records.
- Zero fleet-wide failures.

These can be restored only after the supporting specification, test method or fleet data is approved.

## Information still required

### Essential technical specification

- Confirmed power supply and frequency. The brochure currently contains `[000 V / 00 Hz]`.
- Confirmed pipe connection diameter. The brochure currently contains `[Ø 00 mm]`.
- Confirmed electrical load, enclosure IP rating and applicable CE/UKCA standards.
- Supported pool-volume and flow-rate ranges.
- Pump power/current limits and compatible pump types.
- Salt-cell output/capacity and recommended salinity range.
- UV rating, dose and rated flow.
- Sensor list, measurement ranges, accuracy, calibration and replacement intervals.
- pH/HCl pump output, accuracy and chemical-container capacity.
- Garden zones/outputs and supported irrigation functions.
- Number and type of valve outputs, relays and expansion ports.
- Network requirements, offline behaviour, cloud dependencies and web-app platform support.

### Installation and commercial proof

- Typical and worst-case installation time for new-build and retrofit projects.
- A like-for-like size comparison with the previous prototype to quantify “smaller”.
- Base product price, typical installed price and what is included.
- Evidence for the expected installation-cost reduction.
- Lead time, commissioning process and installer training requirements.
- Warranty, servicing model, spare-parts availability and geographic support coverage.

### Performance and trust proof

- Measurement/update interval.
- Chemical and energy savings data with sample size and methodology.
- Reliability/uptime and installed-fleet data.
- Backwash trigger logic and operational safeguards.
- Alert delivery methods and supply-level sensing method.
- Data security, ownership, retention and remote-access permissions.
- Case studies, installation photography and customer/installer testimonials for KW2000.

## Other launch considerations

- Decide whether garden control is a core headline proposition or a configurable option; the current update treats it as core because the brochure does.
- Confirm whether “HCl dosing” should remain technical copy or be translated into customer language such as “automatic pH correction”.
- Define variants/options if not every installation includes UV, salt cell, spa or garden control.
- Add a downloadable final specification sheet once placeholder values are resolved.
- Add a privacy policy and explain cloud/web-app data handling before collecting production leads.
- Replace “Number coming soon” in the contact menu with the real phone and WhatsApp details before launch.
