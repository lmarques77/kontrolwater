About KONTROLWATER

Traditional pool management relies on schedules — weekly visits, manual testing, reactive fixes. It's a system built around checking in, not staying in control.

KONTROLWATER fixes that. It monitors continuously, calculates precisely, and corrects automatically. Every 10 seconds, without input. The water stays stable. You stay informed, not occupied.
